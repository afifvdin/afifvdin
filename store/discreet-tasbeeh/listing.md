## Description
A quiet tasbeeh counter that stays out of the way.

Tap anywhere to count. Feel a gentle vibration with every tap, so you can keep count without looking. The screen stays pitch black with the number barely visible, so the people around you won't notice.

• Tap anywhere to count
• Haptic feedback on every tap
• Dark, discreet design
• Your count is saved automatically
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking

## Notes for the reviewer (App Review Information → Notes)
Discreet Tasbeeh is a counter for Islamic dhikr (remembrance prayers), typically counted in sets of 33 or 100. The near-black, low-contrast design is intentional so it can be used privately, for example in a mosque or in public. Tap anywhere to increment; the circular-arrow icon at the bottom resets the count to zero.
